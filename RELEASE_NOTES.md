## Threads Cards 1.0

- Paste a Threads post link and the app fetches the post: name, checkmark, picture, full text and counts.
- Story-size cards styled like the Threads share card, with the full text. When the author continued the post in the thread, each part gets its own card (1/2, 2/2...), like Threads. A post too long for one card keeps its text size and continues on the next card, breaking at the end of a sentence; the pills count every card (1/3, 2/3, 3/3).
- **Replies:** paste a reply's link and the post it answers comes first, each card with its own name, picture and checkmark.
- **History** (clock button, top right): every post you get is kept. Tap one to bring it back, swipe left to delete.
- **Save to Photos** puts the card images in your camera roll.
- **Save Video:** when the post has a video (or several), one tap saves it to Photos.
- Every field can be edited (including the thread's next posts), and if the fetch fails you can type or paste the text yourself.
- **Look:** the app's screens use the Soulection style (dark #1A1A1A, thin borders, Bai Jamjuree, white pill buttons). The story cards keep the Threads look.
