## Threads Cards 1.0

- Paste a Threads post link: the app reads the post (name, checkmark, picture and full text) and makes story-size cards that look like the Threads share card.
- **Threads:** each of the author's posts in the thread gets its own card (1/2, 2/2...).
- **Replies:** the post being answered comes first, with its own name, picture and checkmark.
- **Long posts** keep their text size and continue on the next card, breaking at the end of a sentence. Short posts get one card with no pill.
- **Save to Photos** saves every card; **Save Video** saves the post's videos.
- **History** (clock button): every post you get is kept. Tap to reopen, swipe left to delete.
- **Edit post:** change any field before saving, or type the text yourself if a post can't be read.
- **Look:** the app's screens use the Soulection style (dark, thin grey outlines, Bai Jamjuree); the title uses Threads' font. The cards keep the Threads look.
